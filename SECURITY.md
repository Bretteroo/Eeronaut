# Security policy

Let's be safe!

## Environments

Eeronaut is intended to be run on your own trusted local network.  Putting it out on the public internet is not recommended.

If you'd like to manage your Eero network with Eeronaut while away from home, please do so over a VPN, or just use Eero's own mobile apps.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Use GitHub's private reporting: open the repository's **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**, or go straight to <https://github.com/Bretteroo/Eeronaut/security/advisories/new>.  Your report will be hidden from public view.

I want Eeronaut to be a safe and trusted part of your homelab.  Thanks for reporting!
