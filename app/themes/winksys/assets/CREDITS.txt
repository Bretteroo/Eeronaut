Everything in this theme is drawn for it or drawn in CSS, except the
Windows 95 controls, below.

wordmark.png
  The program's name with "Open source browser control" under it, supplied
  by the author and under no third-party license. Its indigo background was
  keyed out to transparent so the one file sits on the band in both
  appearances.

favicon.png
  A small router with two antennas and a row of lights, drawn for this theme
  on a sixteen-pixel grid and scaled up without smoothing.

The theme is an impression of the setup pages home routers shipped with in
the early 2000s, made from memory of the arrangement and a reading of the
colors: no logo, wordmark, image, or text from any of that firmware is used,
and this project is unaffiliated with any router maker. The indigo, the pale
gray, and the black are read off an archived copy of one of those pages.

check.svg, check-off.svg, select.svg, select-down.svg, radio.svg,
radio-off.svg, radio-dot.svg
  The tick, the dropdown button, and the option button from 95.css by Jordan
  Scales and Luka Novak (https://git.envs.net/ljn/95.css), under the MIT
  license in LICENSE-95css.txt, taken out of its stylesheet as files. The
  bevels in theme.css follow the same stylesheet.

check-dark.svg, select-dark.svg, select-down-dark.svg, radio-dark.svg,
radio-dot-dark.svg
  The same glyphs recolored for the dark appearance.
